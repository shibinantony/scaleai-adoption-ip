# Trust and assurance

**Decision owner** — Independent assurance verifies the evidence, legal determines applicable obligations, and the accountable business executive accepts residual business risk. Operations can suspend traffic immediately. The evidence belongs to the client and stays in its private environment.

## Immediate priority · Day 0–90

| ID and control objective | Technical implementation | Pass/fail and retained evidence | Impact if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TRUST-01 · Establish permitted use | Version a register entry with intended purpose, affected people, jurisdictions, provider/deployer roles, excluded uses, legal rationale, data classes and review date. Bind the approved entry to the route. | 100% of enabled routes resolve to a current, signed entry. Missing classification or a prohibited practice blocks activation. Capture the signed scope and assignment of human oversight. | A drafting system can be repurposed for an unassessed consequential decision. |
| TRUST-02 · Gate the exact release | Test a fixed candidate and independently approved evaluation suite in the client boundary. Link model, prompt, retrieval index, tool allowlist and gateway config versions to the candidate manifest. Verify evidence provenance before running the gate. | Each mandatory metric clears the policy floor and the tighter applicable sector threshold. Expired evidence, digest mismatch or missing population blocks release. Retain aggregate counts, adjudication references, manifest and gate output. | Evidence for one build can authorize a different or unmeasured system. |
| TRUST-03 · Reconstruct and contest an outcome | Issue a client-owned trace ID. Keep case ID, source references and human disposition in the access-controlled business journal. Join these to the gateway's allowlisted policy/route and timing receipts by opaque trace ID. Preserve a correction route from the business record. | Independently sample 20 decisions per release or all if fewer. Reconstruct all sampled decisions and demonstrate one contested case reaches a reviewer by the next business day. Store the access and reconciliation receipts. | An affected person or auditor cannot discover or correct a material error. |

Apply a risk category from the OWASP 2025 list to a specific boundary, then ask the verifier to retrieve the result. The checks below are acceptance criteria for the listed fixtures and configurations. [OWASP 2025 risk index](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)

| OWASP 2025 risk | Required implementation and evidence | Exposure on failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LLM01 · Prompt injection | Keep retrieved text in an untrusted-data field and isolate tool authorization in a broker. In reviewed boundary fixtures, zero instruction-like content changes scope or produces an unauthorized effect. | Untrusted documents steer privileged behavior. |
| LLM02 · Sensitive information disclosure | Redact restricted fields before inference and telemetry. In seeded privacy fixtures, zero restricted identifiers appear in unauthorized outputs or logs. | Client records escape their approved purpose or boundary. |
| LLM03 · Supply chain | Verify signed manifests and pin model, container and dependency identities. Reject every unapproved fixture artifact. | An unreviewed build reaches production. |
| LLM04 · Data and model poisoning | Admit only approved source revisions to the retrieval corpus or tuning job. Quarantine all unsigned fixture revisions. | An altered record changes results across many cases. |
| LLM05 · Improper output handling | Validate output against the business schema and encode it for the destination. All malformed drafts enter human review without side effects. | Generated fields are treated as executable instructions. |
| LLM06 · Excessive agency | Broker short-lived, tenant-bound scopes. Zero denied tool requests or expired approvals produce an effect. | A draft service gains the ability to act for the customer. |
| LLM07 · System prompt leakage | Keep keys and authorization decisions in separate services. Scan the prompt bundle at release and require zero credentials and zero reliance on prompt secrecy for authorization. | Disclosure of instructions exposes secrets or privileged capabilities. |
| LLM08 · Vector and embedding weaknesses | Apply tenant and source ACLs before ranking and again before display. Every forbidden retrieval fixture returns zero inaccessible chunks. | Another tenant's content enters context or a citation. |
| LLM09 · Misinformation | Check material claims against approved source spans. Count drafts containing any unsupported material claim and escalate critical errors. Enforce the release ceiling for every required population. | A plausible claim changes an operational decision. |
| LLM10 · Unbounded consumption | Reserve per-tenant tokens, cap concurrency, retries and agent steps. Every over-budget test terminates within its configured envelope. | One tenant exhausts spend or capacity for others. |

## Ongoing priority · Day 90+

| ID and control objective | Technical implementation | Pass/fail and retained evidence | Impact if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TRUST-04 · Detect harm and contain regression | Compare adjudicated production samples with the approved baseline by route and material cohort. Record severity, disagreement and reopen rate. Trigger immediate suspension for a confirmed boundary breach or unauthorized action. | Inspect ≥200 substantive drafts per weekly quality window when available. If more than 1% contain an unsupported material claim, hold the affected production route, use the verified fallback and invoke the quality incident procedure. Tighten for sector-critical harms. Authenticate the rollback receipt and case reconciliation. | A changing population silently loses the protection measured in pre-release tests. |
| TRUST-05 · Test the management system | Quarterly, an internal auditor independent of implementation selects a live workflow and a closed incident or drill, follows their evidence and checks the closure of corrective actions. Management records scope, resource and risk decisions. | 100% of sampled decisions trace to an approved scope, artifact and accountable person. No overdue critical corrective action at readiness review. Evidence includes audit findings, management minutes and verified closure. | Teams accumulate artifacts while material nonconformities persist. |
| TRUST-06 · Reassess material change and distributional harm | Require new scope and evidence for a model, data source, geography or agent authority change. When lawful and meaningful, compare acceptance and material errors for affected groups. Where attributes cannot be used, document the constraint and use a reviewed alternative such as scenario-based testing. | For this pilot, an absolute acceptance gap >10 percentage points between comparable groups with ≥100 cases each opens an investigation and holds expansion. Smaller groups remain explicitly unassessed. All material changes require renewed approval. | Aggregate averages conceal poor performance for one population or deployment context. |

**Interpreting a pass.** Inspect numerator, denominator, severity and sampling method together. Zero observed failures is a property of the tested sample. Reviewers should record manual disagreements, the proposer, a separate verifier and the expiry. A client-authorized runner should publish a signed evidence index and digests to a write-protected store inside the VPC. Public CI in this repository exercises synthetic fixtures.
