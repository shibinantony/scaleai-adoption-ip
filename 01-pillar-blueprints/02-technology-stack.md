# Technology stack and model routing

The head of AI engineering approves a route for a named workflow, data class and model revision. For the invoice-exception pilot, the service can read an authorized case, retrieve current purchasing policy and draft a cited explanation. An accounts-payable reviewer decides what to release. Posting to the ledger, changing supplier details and initiating payment stay with the existing business process.

## Immediate priority · Day 0–90

**Handoff.** Give the workflow owner a route manifest, an evaluation report by exception type and a tested manual fallback. Keep inference, embeddings, retrieval and evaluation inside the client VPC or disconnected enclave. Reject routes that allow third-party training on client inputs or outputs.

### Select a route

1. **Can a rule settle the question?** Recalculate totals, currency and invoice-to-order variance in decimal arithmetic against the system of record. Return those verified values to the reviewer. A language model may explain a discrepancy.
2. **Do the sources support an answer?** Retrieve with the authenticated tenant, case permissions and policy effective date. Missing evidence, conflicting effective policies or a revoked document sends the case to manual review. A larger model cannot restore missing authority.
3. **Is the exception covered by the small model's qualification report?** Use the approved domain-tuned small language model (SLM) for routine classification in covered languages. A fixed template can turn a verified classification and source into a reviewer draft. A frozen holdout evaluates each route, including a class that must abstain. Model self-confidence has no role in authorization.
4. **Does the case match an approved complex reasoning class?** Use the separately qualified larger or frontier model only if the same tenant boundary, training prohibition, budget and approval path hold. If this route is unavailable, mark the case for manual handling. Apply the same output schema and citation checks on both routes.

|---|---|---|---|
| **TECH-01 · Admit only qualified routes.** AI platform lead | Bind workflow, exception class, residency, model digest, prompt hash and resource cap to a signed route manifest. Exercise every rule, default and disabled route with a recorded decision. | Every enabled route has a current approval and matching digest. All unlisted classes and unavailable routes land in the manual queue. Zero requests reach an unapproved endpoint. | A difficult case silently moves to an unqualified model or another processing boundary. |
| **TECH-02 · Ground material claims.** Knowledge owner | Store document owner, version, effective dates and access policy with each chunk. Filter before search and before assembly. Emit source ID, version and passage for each substantive claim. Validate references mechanically. Reviewers check that each passage supports the claim. | All cited passages resolve under the original entitlement. At most 1% of at least 200 reviewed substantive drafts contain any unsupported material claim. All missing-authority fixtures abstain. | A plausible explanation cites the wrong policy period or another customer's record. |
| **TECH-03 · Preserve human authority.** Accounts-payable controller | Keep the model-facing workflow read-only. The reviewer sees original amounts, cited passages, proposed text and uncertainty flags. Record reviewer identity, case revision and disposition in the approval system. | Every released draft has a human decision bound to that revision. Zero model identities can post, pay or change a supplier. Every edited draft invalidates its earlier approval. | An incorrect draft can alter a payable or authorize a disbursement. |

Count a draft once when any material claim lacks support. Stratify the review by route and exception type. Separately report abstentions divided by all eligible cases, so excess deferrals remain visible even when the unsupported-claim rate falls. The purchasing-policy owner and controller resolve disputed labels before signing the report. NIST identifies confabulation and recommends evaluating outputs and their sources. **Worked routing decision.** Two current records show a synthetic invoice for 1,075.00 and an order for 1,000.00 in the same currency. The rules engine records a 75.00 variance. A private SLM classifies the exception and a fixed template renders the variance and policy citation. A covered exception involving several compatible policy clauses may take the private larger-model drafting route. If the records use different currencies and the case lacks an approved exchange-rate source, the reviewer receives the records and an escalation reason.

## Ongoing priority · Day 90+

**Handoff.** The platform lead submits a side-by-side route comparison, a bounded agent design and a rollback rehearsal before requesting another workflow or tenant.

| Control and accountable owner | Implementation and retained evidence | Scale pass condition | Blast radius if omitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TECH-04 · Qualify a replacement model.** Model evaluation lead | Shadow a candidate inside the same boundary on the same labeled cases. Blind reviewers to model identity. Compare acceptance, errors, abstentions, latency and full cost per accepted outcome by exception class. Archive both manifests and paired results. | Both routes pass the release gate independently. For this pilot, the candidate's acceptance rate per eligible case falls by at most 2 percentage points versus the incumbent on at least 200 paired eligible cases. The workflow owner signs the cost and capacity trade-off. | Portfolio averages hide an expensive regression in a minority exception class. |
| **TECH-05 · Bound multi-agent work.** Orchestration lead | Introduce separate retrieval and drafting identities with an explicit state transition table. Give each a fixed read scope. The coordinator verifies outputs and owns a single deadline and shared call budget across agents and retries. | Every transition is allowlisted. In a synthetic budget-exhaustion drill, the next call is denied and exactly one manual case is created. Zero delegated identities can increase the parent's scope. | A chain can repeat work, multiply charges or gain authority at a handoff. |
| **TECH-06 · Detect and reverse quality drift.** Service owner | Compare weekly reviewer labels, abstentions and exception mix with the signed baseline. Version model, prompt, index, rules and routing as one release. Reprocess affected open drafts under the last approved compatible release. | Review each seven-day cohort once it has 200 substantive drafts. Until then, accumulate cases and hold expansion. A breach of the approved error ceiling freezes the affected route. A timed drill restores the approved release or manual handling within 15 minutes. | A stale index or changed input population keeps producing incorrect drafts after a model rollback. |

The service owner records the baseline, denominators, route changes and rollback timestamps in the private release record. Cross-tenant exposure or an unauthorized action suspends the affected path immediately regardless of sample size. Business acceptance and unit-cost calculations use the same case ledger as Finance.
