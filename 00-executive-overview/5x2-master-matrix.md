# Five pillars, from the first release to expansion

## The director’s record

**At intake**, write down the workflow, tenant, intended use, excluded actions, eligible volume and spending cap. Name two people who can suspend it. [Discovery diagnostic](../03-client-execution-kit/day-0-discovery-diagnostic.md)

**At release**, identify the exact deployment, the independent reviewer’s finding, the business risk owner, the permitted cases, the rollback target and the approval expiry. If evidence is missing, stale or from a different deployment, hold the release. [Day 0–90 runbook](../03-client-execution-kit/day-0-90-immediate-runbook.md)

**At expansion**, add the actual cost and mix of cases, incidents, outstanding fixes and the proposed change. A data incident sends the affected workload back to the manual queue. A cost overrun holds the next investment while the team works within its existing authorization. [Day 90+ operating guide](../03-client-execution-kit/day-90-plus-ongoing-runbook.md)
