# Five pillars across two investment horizons

The steering group uses this matrix at intake, at the Day 90 review and before each expansion. Each cell names the artifact that changes hands and the evidence its owner must supply. Day 90 is a decision meeting. A team can keep operating inside an approved canary while it gathers enough settled outcomes for an expansion decision.

| Pillar and accountable lead | Immediate priority · Day 0–90 · bound the first deployment | Ongoing priority · Day 90+ · earn the next expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Process and FinOps](../01-pillar-blueprints/01-process-and-finops.md) · COO | Redesign one queue, baseline manual cost, agree the acceptance rubric, give reviewers the final action and fund a manual fallback. Hand over a workflow charter and settled-case ledger. | Compare cost and acceptance across matched case types. Fund a second workload after the first clears both value gates. Publish a monthly margin bridge showing where finance can realize savings. |
| [Technology](../01-pillar-blueprints/02-technology-stack.md) · AI platform lead | Benchmark a private small language model against approved alternatives. Ground drafts in authorized sources and abstain when evidence is missing. Hand over the versioned route and evaluation set. | Admit a new model or bounded agent step through a paired comparison, a resource envelope and an independent regression decision. Retain the last known-good route for rollback. |
| [Tools](../01-pillar-blueprints/03-tooling-ecosystem.md) · platform operations lead | Authenticate, meter and redact at the gateway. Exercise deterministic guardrails on a fixed set of cases. Demonstrate receipt of a client-owned audit event and delivery of a blocked request to the manual queue. | Re-run challenge suites and drift checks after each material change. Reconcile monitoring with gateway receipts, drill lost telemetry and revoke tools that lack current evidence. |
| [Architecture](../01-pillar-blueprints/04-reference-architecture.md) · enterprise architect | Document the data boundary and deploy the control and data planes separately. Show denial of cross-tenant access and public egress in the chosen VPC or air-gapped profile. | Rehearse isolation and recovery with a second synthetic tenant. Test scoped Model Context Protocol grants, revocation and independent tenant rollback before new access opens. |
| [Trust and assurance](../01-pillar-blueprints/05-trust-and-assurance.md) · independent assurance lead | Classify the use, map obligations to controls, verify candidate-bound evidence and obtain human approval. Deliver the signed decision and indexed evidence in client custody. | Repeat evaluation on change, sample decisions back to source and verify remediation. Management reviews residual risk and corrective actions before pursuing an external audit. |

## What the director signs

At intake, record the workflow, tenant, intended use, excluded actions, eligible volume, cost cap and two named individuals who can suspend service. At release, record the actual deployment identity, assurance verdict, risk owner, allowed cohort, rollback target and decision expiry. At expansion, add unit economics, workload mix, material incidents, open actions and the proposed change. Reopening a closed decision requires evidence for the new scope.

The gate fails closed when evidence is missing, stale or outside the candidate scope. A security incident moves the affected workflow to its manual queue. A commercial shortfall holds further investment while the team investigates under the existing authorization.
