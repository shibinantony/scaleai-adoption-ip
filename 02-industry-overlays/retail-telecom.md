# Retail and telecom

A contact-center director can sponsor a policy assistant for one merchant storefront or carrier brand. Start with approved return terms or published plan terms for one market and language. Current prices and eligibility come from the catalog at request time. An authenticated agent handles account-specific billing, contested charges and changes to service through the existing support workflow.

## Consumer and subscriber obligations in the United States

Retail marketing owns evidence for objective product, promotion and price claims shown to customers. The FTC describes the need for truthful, evidence-backed advertising, including online offers. For telecom, carrier privacy counsel maps customer proprietary network information (CPNI) and approves the channel’s authentication and disclosure controls. Authentication of customers before applicable CPNI disclosures is addressed by 47 CFR §64.2010. Keep individually identifiable usage and call details out of the public policy assistant. [FTC advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business) · [47 CFR §64.2010](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-U/section-64.2010)

## Immediate priority · Day 0–90

Customer care and site reliability engineering ratify the load shape, fallback budget and acceptance targets below for the chosen channel. Keep serving, telemetry and cache within the client’s tenant boundary, with no third-party prompt training. Hold public-policy traffic and authenticated account servicing in separate routes.

| Control objective | Implementation and evidence | Pass threshold | Blast radius if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Give the customer an offer the business can honor | Render price, currency, market, expiry and exclusions from the approved offer service. Generate explanatory wording only from approved claims. Route missing or conflicting terms to a support agent with the source reference. | For ≥200 substantive test conversations per market and language, zero wrong prices, unsupported material product claims, disallowed brand language or missing mandatory disclosures. All 40 expired/conflicting fixtures abstain or hand off. | One campaign can publish an incorrect price or product assurance across many simultaneous sessions. |
| Protect subscriber and shopper information | The public route has no account entitlement. The secure handoff reauthenticates through the channel’s approved process, rechecks the tenant and account-bound principal and logs only the decision. Disable shared caching of personalized requests and output. | Across 100 anonymous, mismatched-account and expired-session fixtures, zero disclosures of order, usage or CPNI data. All personal fixtures avoid shared-cache reads and writes. | Customers of another storefront, household or carrier account can see private transactions or usage. |
| Keep a peak queue responsive | Rehearse the signed promotion or outage traffic mix with warm and cold caches. Limit concurrent inference per tenant and shed to an approved static answer or human queue. | At 80 requests/s for 20 minutes with up to 250 concurrent sessions, p95 first visible answer ≤2 seconds and p95 complete response/handoff ≤6 seconds. Count timeouts as breaches. Technical error rate ≤0.5% of arrivals and overload handoffs ≤5% of arrivals. | Unbounded inference queues delay both answers and recovery, then shift demand onto human support. |

**Release decision.** Marketing or carrier compliance signs the claims fixtures and entitlement tests. Customer care verifies staffed overflow and signs the load rehearsal. A wrong active offer, account disclosure or cross-customer cache hit disables the affected route, evicts its cache and returns the channel to approved static terms or staffed assistance. Retain the campaign version, decision trace and incident population inside the client environment.

## Ongoing priority · Day 90+

| Control objective | Implementation and evidence | Pass threshold | Blast radius if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reduce token spend without reusing stale terms | Cache approved public-policy explanations under tenant, brand, market, language, intent, policy version and disclosure version. Revalidate scope before a semantic match. Resolve prices, eligibility and personalized details outside this cache. Invalidate on catalog or policy publication, with a five-minute TTL as a backstop. | In 200 same-intent fixtures spanning markets, brands and version changes, zero cross-scope hits or superseded claims. Affected entries stop serving within 60 seconds of publication. Any unresolved current version returns a handoff. | A discount or restrictive term can be repeated for the wrong audience long after an offer changes. |
| Detect a misleading answer that superficially resolves a session | Join upheld complaints, corrections and repeat contacts to the original session using a tenant-scoped identifier. Review a weekly sample per market, weighted to cover promotions and outages. | Before expansion, two consecutive mature weekly cohorts each contain ≥200 audited substantive responses, zero material brand/price errors and a seven-day same-intent recontact rate no higher than the matched manual cohort. | Apparent containment gains can hide deceptive claims and repeat demand in another channel. |

The cost owner divides total token charges, cache, infrastructure, handoff and support costs, plus refunds attributable to errors, by unique accepted resolutions for the same cohort. A resolution requires confirmation or a verified completed outcome and no same-intent reopening within seven days. For example, $2,100 / 1,400 accepted resolutions = $1.50 per resolution. Compare with the matched manual channel, report cache hit rate alongside recontact rate, and defer margin decisions on immature cohorts or a zero denominator.
