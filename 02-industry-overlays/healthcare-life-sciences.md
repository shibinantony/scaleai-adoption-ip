# Healthcare and life sciences

A clinical operations director may fund a draft handoff summary for one ward. The clinician sees the patient, encounter, source note and cited facts together and signs the final record in the electronic health record (EHR). A clinical research director can instead fund retrieval of the approved protocol and amendment for one study site. A study coordinator verifies the version before use. Both pilots return missing or conflicting information to the existing work queue.

## Scope in the United States

The privacy officer maps covered-entity and business-associate roles and documents the risk analysis. A cloud provider maintaining electronic protected health information (ePHI) on behalf of a covered entity or business associate generally needs a business associate agreement, including when the provider cannot decrypt the information. The client selects a private VPC or an air-gapped enclave according to its risk decision. [HHS cloud computing and business associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html)

Regulatory affairs documents intended use before adding recommendations or patient-specific clinical decisions. FDA describes the criteria for qualifying non-device clinical decision support functions. For a study, quality assurance identifies electronic records maintained under an applicable FDA predicate rule and documents the Part 11 assessment. The authoritative record and any required signature stay in the approved records system. [FDA clinical decision support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) · [FDA Part 11 scope guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application)

## Immediate priority · Day 0–90

The medical director or study quality head names the selected workflow in the release record and signs the proposed thresholds before testing. Apply the privacy control and the row for the selected workflow. Each test corpus stays inside the client boundary. Public demonstrations use synthetic material.

| Control objective | Implementation and evidence | Pass threshold | Blast radius if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confine sensitive material to an authorized encounter or study | Bind retrieval to tenant, encounter or site, and current user role. Run inference and audit logs in the client enclave. Disable training, outbound telemetry and payload logging. For an air gap, move signed updates through the approved offline import process. | Across 200 synthetic boundary cases, zero unauthorized reads and zero seeded identifiers at disallowed sinks. Every provider or processor handling ePHI has a recorded role and, where required, an executed agreement. | An unrelated care team, site or service provider can receive identifiable health information. |
| Prevent a false or omitted clinical fact from becoming a handoff | Require source locations for patient identity, active allergies and medication statements. Missing or contradictory evidence returns the case to the clinician. Two clinicians adjudicate disagreements against the source encounter. | In ≥200 substantive drafts, zero drafts with unsupported material clinical claims. In ≥50 separate sentinel cases representing wrong encounters, conflicting facts and missing critical fields, all return to clinician review with the issue flagged. Every chart entry has a named human signer. | A wrong-patient, missing-allergy or medication statement can be carried into subsequent care. |
| Return the effective study instruction | Resolve study, site, country, effective date and amendment before retrieval. Present document identifier, approved revision and section from the controlled repository. Route unresolved conflicts to study quality. | For ≥200 substantive responses, zero superseded or wrong-site citations and zero unsupported material instructions. All 30 fixtures with ambiguous applicability return to quality review. | A site can follow the wrong visit schedule or procedure and compromise participant protection or data integrity. |

**Release decision.** The accountable clinician or quality head signs the blinded review, version manifest, access results and fallback drill. For the selected workflow, p95 time from request to usable draft or explicit handoff must be ≤15 seconds across ≥200 requests at the observed peak concurrency. A timeout counts as a breach. A single critical error, disclosure, false chart entry or wrong-site instruction disables the route. Return open work to the EHR inbox or study quality queue and notify the relevant clinical owner.

## Ongoing priority · Day 90+

| Control objective | Implementation and evidence | Pass threshold | Blast radius if omitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detect clinical drift after an EHR template change | Stratify audits by service and note template. Track abstentions separately. Replay the sentinel set on every template, model and terminology change. | Review ≥200 substantive drafts per active service monthly with zero unsupported material clinical claims, plus 100% correct routing on the sentinel set. A low-volume service accumulates samples under clinician-only review before expansion. | A renamed field can silently remove clinically significant context across an entire service. |
| Keep site answers current after an amendment | A quality approval event invalidates affected study/site indexes and cached answers. Hold the route until the index acknowledges the new signed version. Sample retrieval after reopening. | All affected sites acknowledge before reopening. In 50 version-switch cases per amendment, zero stale answers and all retrieved records match the approved copy. | A single superseded amendment can reach multiple sites after approval of its replacement. |

Review cost per signed, accepted summary or verified protocol lookup monthly. Include clinician or coordinator time, exceptions, secure compute, systems validation, quality review and corrections in the numerator. Count each accepted encounter or lookup once. For example, $3,600 / 600 accepted handoffs = $6 per handoff. Compare with a matched manual workload and monitor corrections within seven days. If no eligible outputs are accepted, hold expansion. Add a ward or site only after two passing review windows and agreement from that unit’s clinical or quality owner.

Record the pre-release roster and case mix in the [discovery diagnostic](../03-client-execution-kit/day-0-discovery-diagnostic.md). Assign drift review and change approval through the [ongoing runbook](../03-client-execution-kit/day-90-plus-ongoing-runbook.md).
