# Day 0–90 · Deliver the first controlled workflow

**Mandate.** In thirteen weekly increments, qualify one high-value workflow for a named tenant. The reference case drafts an accounts-payable invoice exception. A human owns the disposition, the ledger and any payment. A second workflow can follow after satisfying the same gates against its own purpose, economics and evidence. An unmet gate extends the prior authorized phase.

| Window | Accountable lead and action | Exit evidence and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 · days 0–6 | Sponsor convenes the five-pillar diagnostic, identifies affected users and ranks candidate queues with Finance. | Signed pilot charter names the accountable owner, scope, cost cap and fallback. Hold any candidate without permitted data access. |
| Week 2 · days 7–13 | Process owner retrieves four historical weeks of matched cases or extends discovery until the baseline is available, defines a settled accepted outcome, and identifies where duplicate entry or rework starts. | CFO signs cost buckets, case-mix strata, baseline volume and review capacity. Stop if the unit of value cannot be measured. |
| Week 3 · days 14–20 | Security and legal identify client data classes, permitted processing location, provider terms and applicable obligations. | CISO signs the perimeter map, egress policy, no-training condition, key custody and log ownership. Use synthetic records during engineering. |
| Week 4 · days 21–27 | Platform lead deploys the private gateway, caller identity binding, token reservation, denied public egress and a client-owned audit sink. | Demonstrate allowed and denied requests for two synthetic tenants. Inspect all permitted sinks for seeded restricted fields. Keep live data out until perimeter tests pass. |
| Week 5 · days 28–34 | Data owner scopes retrieval by user, tenant, case and effective date. Finance systems team removes agent writes from the architecture. | ACL-revocation test passes. Source links resolve for the reviewer. A forbidden tool request produces an auditable denial and zero side effects. |
| Week 6 · days 35–41 | Model lead qualifies small and larger private routes on comparable exception types and freezes the candidate bundle. | Select least-cost eligible route per task within quality and latency limits. Missing source support produces an abstention and a manual case. |
| Week 7 · days 42–48 | Assurance lead defines material errors, severe harm, sector slices and minimum sample sizes with domain reviewers. Separate tuning cases from evaluation cases. | Independent reviewers adjudicate disagreements and approve the evaluation suite digest. Save the rationale for thresholds before scoring the candidate. |
| Week 8 · days 49–55 | Release engineer runs the policy gate on authenticated evidence. Operations drills a failed model, unavailable evidence sink and manual failover. | Retain the gate result, manual reconciliation and timed rollback of ≤15 minutes. A failed required control holds the candidate in shadow. |
| Week 9 · days 56–62 | Service owner runs private shadow traffic under approved data terms. Compare drafts with independently completed manual cases. | Reconcile arrivals, timeouts, abstentions, drafts and review cost. Independent assurance and CISO sign the evidence for this candidate. |
| Week 10 · days 63–69 | COO opens a supervised canary only after release approval. Rerun the gate at promotion with evidence still current for this candidate and scope (168 hours in the starter policy). Renew expired evidence before running the gate. Start with one staffed shift, one tenant, at most 5% of eligible cases and 50 cases/day. | Every release has a human decision. Monitor all canary receipts, guardrail outcomes, intake/backlog and spend daily. On a hard stop, route the cohort to people immediately. |
| Week 11 · days 70–76 | Operations reviews the first settled cases at seven days. Rerun the gate with current evidence before any traffic increase. If required controls hold, seek a signed increase to at most 25% and 200 cases/day in the same scope. | Record sample size, error severity, reopen rate and reviewer queue age by case type. An undersized sample keeps the cohort within its prior limits. |
| Week 12 · days 77–83 | Finance prices all activity, including failed attempts and manual completions. Independently verify evidence for the proposed operating scope. | Provide settled-case ledger, updated cost forecast, incident list and deployment fingerprint. Compare acceptance and cost with the signed business thresholds. |
| Week 13 · days 84–90 | Director chairs a decision with COO, CIO, CISO, finance and independent assurance. Confirm production support and suspension authority. | Sign release, continued canary, hold or retirement for a named scope. Record budget, case cap, residual risk, expiry and next review. Unsettled or insufficient evidence can extend the canary. |

## What must be in the release record

Keep the scope and legal decision, dated diagnostic, baseline ledger, route and artifact digests, synthetic or client-controlled evaluation results, signed evaluator identity, pre-approved suite digest, all fail/deny receipts, rollback drill and business authorization. The reviewer must be able to trace each item from workflow through candidate to evidence owner. Public examples contain only synthetic information. [Diagnostic](day-0-discovery-diagnostic.md) · [Policy kit and execution instructions](../04-policy-as-code-templates/README.md)

Confirmed data-boundary failures or unauthorized actions suspend the affected route. Quarantine affected drafts, preserve restricted evidence and assign all open case IDs to the existing manual queue. The CISO and workflow owner verify the fix and commission a fresh evaluation before any reopening. Financial underperformance holds expansion and returns the cost model to Finance.
