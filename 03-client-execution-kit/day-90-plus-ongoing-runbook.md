# Day 90+ · Operate and expand

**Mandate.** Keep an approved workflow inside its authorized purpose, risk limits and budget. Each new tenant, agent capability, data source or decision right needs its own exposure assessment and approval. The service owner runs this cadence. The business sponsor pays for review, fallback and assurance as well as inference.

| Cadence and owner | Observation and decision rule | Action and retained evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each request · platform on-call | Check authenticated tenant, fresh entitlement, permitted route, remaining budget and the availability of the required audit path. | Deny a missing prerequisite, suppress side effects, issue a client-owned decision receipt and route recoverable cases to the manual queue. A confirmed boundary breach or unauthorized action suspends the affected path immediately. |
| Daily · workflow owner | Reconcile eligible case IDs to pending, assisted and manual dispositions. Compare queue age with the approved service target. | Investigate every unexplained case and repeated queue breach before adding traffic. Use the original case ID on replay. Retain counts, oldest case age and reviewer capacity. |
| Weekly · independent quality lead | For each affected route and material cohort, compare unsupported-claim and abstention rates with the baseline. Accumulate ≥200 substantive drafts per quality decision. Track the eligible population, exclusions and severity separately. | A confirmed critical error invokes incident review immediately. Above 1% unsupported material claims or a tighter sector ceiling, hold the affected route, revert to a verified compatible version or manual review, and repeat the gate. Low volume holds expansion. |
| Weekly · finance partner | Reconcile actual cost and accepted outcomes on settled cohorts. Recheck route mix and seven-day reopens. Investigate two consecutive eligible weeks above 80% of matched manual unit cost or below 85% acceptance. | Freeze new allocation. Separate price, volume, mix, repeat requests and reviewer time. A cheaper route must requalify under the same quality floor. Store the signed cost bridge. |
| Each material change · release manager | Bind a fresh evidence manifest to the exact model, prompt, policy, index, tool permissions and deployment scope. Exercise changed behavior alongside prior regression cases. | An independent assessor reviews evidence provenance and disposition. Promote only the approved candidate. Revoke the old manifest after cutover and retain a compatible rollback bundle. |
| Monthly · platform lead | Revoke a synthetic tenant, rehearse restoration, simulate unavailable dependencies and check the on-call handoff. | Prove all open case IDs move exactly once and service returns to an authorized route within 15 minutes. Restore failures freeze scope expansion. Record the drill and corrective-action owner. |
| Quarterly · accountable executive | Review service value, complaint trends, subgroup outcomes where appropriate, legal changes and material incidents. Independent internal audit traces a sample to the release and the human action. | Approve a bounded expansion, continue, reduce or retire. Record audit scope, open risks, resources, owners and deadlines. Close overdue critical actions before an ISO/IEC 42001 readiness decision. |

## Drift response and restart

1. **Detect and size.** Sample shifts in input class, language, abstention and source freshness by tenant and route. For the invoice pilot, a ≥10 percentage-point shift in case-type share against the prior four-week baseline triggers analyst review. An unknown denominator blocks a claim that quality held.
2. **Contain.** On a hard boundary failure, revoke the affected tool grant, disable the route and preserve a restricted incident snapshot immediately. Page security and the workflow owner. Quarantine affected drafts and reconcile the open queue to people. Assess notification duties with counsel under the applicable incident timetable.
3. **Diagnose.** Compare the candidate manifest with the last good bundle. Inspect changes in corpus membership, entitlements, prompt/model versions, evaluator, traffic mix and reviewer labels. Record impact by time range and potentially affected case IDs inside the client environment.
4. **Verify.** Re-adjudicate impacted cases, repair the cause and replay regression and boundary fixtures. Require new authenticated gate evidence and a successful time-bounded restoration test. Independent assurance verifies both.
5. **Restart.** Security and the workflow owner authorize a small supervised cohort, the service owner confirms staffing, and finance confirms the budget. Expand only after the signed observation window settles. Index incident, affected decisions, corrective action and release record together.

## Adding a second agent or tenant

First run the proposed scope in shadow against a matched, authorized cohort. Set a single maximum step, token and time budget for the entire agent chain, bind each step to its own minimal identity, and give any final business action to a human. The architecture owner demonstrates that one tenant can be suspended without stopping the other. Compare accepted outcomes, unsupported claims, subgroup effects, latency and fully loaded cost. Return to the steering group with the independent gate verdict and the incremental budget.

If the board retires the workflow, route open cases to the manual team, revoke secrets and tool grants, stop billing reservations and expire tenant artifacts according to the approved record schedule and legal holds. The accountable owner signs case reconciliation and a final cost statement.
